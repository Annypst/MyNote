Describe a time when you saw a lot of people smiling.

I’d like to talk about my high school graduation ceremony, which was one of the happiest moments I’ve ever experienced, and almost everyone was smiling warmly that day.

I saw smiles everywhere. My classmates were smiling and taking photos with each other. Some were laughing happily, while others had gentle, quiet smiles. Even though some of us felt a little sad to leave school, most smiles were full of joy and pride.

I also noticed my teachers smiling at us. They looked satisfied and proud, because we had finally graduated after years of hard work. My parents were smiling too, as they were really happy for my achievement.

For me, this event was really meaningful. The smiles weren’t just about being happy; they showed relief, excitement and hope for the future. We were about to start a new life and go to university. That day’s smiles made me feel warm and thankful. It’s a memory I will never forget.
中文大意

我想说的是我的高中毕业典礼，那是我经历过最开心的时刻之一，那天几乎每个人都在微笑。

当时我刚读完高中。我们所有人都穿着学士服、戴着学士帽。整个礼堂坐满了学生、家长和老师。

我到处都能看到笑容。同学们互相微笑、拍照。有些人笑得很开心，有些人是温柔安静的笑。虽然我们有点舍不得离开学校，但大多数笑容都充满喜悦和自豪。

我也看到老师们对我们微笑，他们看起来很欣慰、很骄傲，因为我们经过多年努力终于毕业了。我父母也在笑，他们为我感到高兴。

对我来说，这个场合很有意义。这些微笑不只是开心，还包含了释然、兴奋和对未来的希望。我们即将开始新生活、去上大学。那天的微笑让我觉得温暖、感恩，是我永远不会忘记的回忆。
